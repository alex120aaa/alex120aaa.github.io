    <h2 class="scut-info-box-title">BIO</h2>
    <div class="scut-info-box-content">
      <p>Qiuxia Wu is an Associate Professor in the School of Software Engineering at South China University of Technology (SCUT), Guangzhou, China. Her research interests focus on intelligent algorithms and software, multimedia technology, video content retrieval, biometric recognition, and biomedical image processing.</p>
      <p>She received her Ph.D. from the School of Automation, South China University of Technology in 2012, and was a visiting scholar at the University of Sydney from 2009 to 2011. After graduation, she joined SCUT as a faculty member, working at the Guangzhou Modern Industrial Technology Research Institute and later the School of Software Engineering. She is an IEEE member and serves as Secretary of the IEEE SMC Guangzhou Chapter. She has served as a reviewer for IEEE TSMC, IET Image Processing, IEEE Signal Processing Letters, DICTA, and other international journals and conferences. According to Scopus, she has authored 63 documents with over 1,300 citations by 1,109 documents, an h-index of 16, and 96 co-authors.</p>
