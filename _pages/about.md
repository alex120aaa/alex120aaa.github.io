    <h2 class="scut-info-box-title">BIO</h2>
    <div class="scut-info-box-content">
      <p>Qiuxia Wu is an Associate Professor in the School of Software Engineering at South China University of Technology (SCUT), Guangzhou, China. Her research interests focus on 3D point cloud analysis, content-based video retrieval, biometrics recognition, and biomedical image analysis.</p>
      <p>She received her Ph.D. from South China University of Technology, supervised by Prof. Feiqi Deng and Prof. David Dagan Feng. She has been a faculty member at SCUT since 2017. Her work has been published in top-tier venues including AAAI, CVPR, ICME, IEEE TCSVT, IEEE TIFS, and Computer Vision and Image Understanding (CVIU). According to Scopus, she has authored 63 documents with over 1,300 citations and an h-index of 16.</p>
