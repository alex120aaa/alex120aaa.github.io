RESEARCH INTERESTS

Dr. Wu's research interests center on 3D point cloud analysis, content-based video retrieval, biometrics recognition, and biomedical image analysis. Her recent work focuses on rotation-invariant representation learning, self-supervised masked autoencoders for point clouds, point cloud completion, and spatio-temporal modelling for action and gait recognition. She is also active in remote sensing segmentation, garment simulation, and medical imaging (e.g., bladder cancer muscle invasion classification). Her research has been published in top-tier venues such as CVPR, AAAI, MICCAI, ICASSP, ICME, IEEE TCSVT, IEEE TIFS, IEEE T-BIOM, and Computer Vision and Image Understanding (CVIU).
